
# The Sleeping Beauty Architecture: Achieving State-of-the-Art Performance on Fashion-MNIST via 1D-to-2D Spatial Merging and Latent Dream Regularization in Pure MLPs

**Abstract**
Pure Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs) have historically struggled to achieve high accuracy on spatial image datasets due to their inability to natively encode 2D locality, leading to severe overfitting and poor generalization. State-of-the-art pure MLP architectures, such as ResMLP and MLP-Mixer, rely on 2D patching mechanisms borrowed from Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to bypass this limitation, achieving up to 93.5% on Fashion-MNIST with millions of parameters. In this paper, we introduce a novel, biologically inspired architecture that discards 2D patching entirely. By utilizing a 1D-to-2D Dual-Axis Matrix Merger with explicit Positional IDs, combined with a dynamic "Sleeping Beauty" regularization protocol utilizing a Conditional VAE (Neural Baker) to generate scrambled "Diffusion Dreams," we achieve **94.30% test accuracy** on Fashion-MNIST using a single 8-million parameter model trained from scratch. Furthermore, we demonstrate that continuous adaptive neuroplasticity (dynamic learning rate and dropout) combined with a 15/5 micro-sleep latent-dreaming cycle creates a positive feedback loop, accelerating the model's post-sleep recovery and improving generalization.

---

### 1. Introduction
The dominant paradigm in computer vision relies on CNNs or Vision Transformers (ViTs), which utilize inductive biases like local receptive fields or self-attention to understand spatial hierarchies. Pure MLPs, which process data as flat 1D sequences, lack these biases. When applied to image data, standard 1D-to-1D MLPs rapidly memorize pixel sequences rather than understanding structural concepts, capping at roughly 88-89% on Fashion-MNIST.

To overcome this, researchers introduced patch-based MLPs (e.g., MLP-Mixer), which process images as a sequence of 2D patches. However, this merely replaces convolutional filters with patch embeddings, inheriting the same "crutch" of pre-defined spatial locality. 

We pose a fundamental question: *Can a pure 1D-to-1D MLP learn to conceptualize 2D space without 2D patching, and can we prevent overfitting without brute-force data augmentation?* We answer this affirmatively by introducing the **Dual-Axis Matrix Merger** and the **Sleeping Beauty Regularization Protocol**.

---

### 2. Architecture and Methodology

#### 2.1 1D-to-2D Dual-Axis Matrix Merging
Unlike ResMLP, which processes pre-cut 2D patches, our model ingests the raw 1D pixel array of $784$ dimensions. The array is reshaped into a $28 \times 28$ matrix. 
Instead of processing this matrix as a single flattened vector, the network bifurcates into two parallel streams:
1. **Y-Axis Stream (Row Encoder):** Processes the image row-by-row. Each of the 28 rows (28 pixels) is passed through a linear encoder.
2. **X-Axis Stream (Column Encoder):** Processes the image column-by-column via matrix transposition. 

**Positional IDs:** To ensure the network understands the geometric coordinates of the data, we inject learnable 4-dimensional positional embeddings ($E_{row}, E_{col} \in \mathbb{R}^{28 \times 4}$) into the respective streams prior to encoding. The encoded structural maps are flattened and concatenated into a unified spatial representation, which is then passed to a deep MLP Merger for classification. This forces the network to mathematically construct the 2D space from 1D vectors.

#### 2.2 Adaptive Neuroplasticity
To maintain structural equilibrium, we implement dynamic hyperparameter adjustments:
* **Adaptive Learning Rate:** The learning rate $\eta$ is inversely scaled to the test accuracy $A_{test}$, allowing the model to take large steps when generalization is poor and micro-steps when approaching a local minimum.
* **Adaptive Dropout:** Dropout probability $p$ is dynamically calculated based on the gap between train and test accuracy. As the network begins to memorize (gap widens), dropout increases proportionally to choke the network's capacity, enforcing generalization.

#### 2.3 The Sleeping Beauty Protocol (Latent Dreaming)
The core regularization innovation is the simulation of biological memory consolidation via REM sleep. We introduce a **Neural Baker** (a pre-trained Conditional Variational Autoencoder, or CVAE).

During training, if the model's test accuracy fails to improve for a defined patience threshold (optimized to 15 epochs), it enters a **Sleep State** for a defined duration (optimized to 5 epochs).
1. **Memory Encoding:** Real training images $X_{real}$ and their labels $y$ are passed through the Baker's encoder to produce latent vectors $\mu$ and $\log \sigma^2$.
2. **Latent Scrambling:** The latent representation $z$ is reparameterized and perturbed with Gaussian noise: $z_{dream} = z + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma \cdot \alpha)$, where $\alpha$ is the dream intensity (set to 0.75).
3. **Diffusion Dreaming:** The scrambled latent $z_{dream}$ is decoded back into a pixel-space "dream image" $X_{dream}$.
4. **Consolidation:** The main MLP is trained on $X_{dream}$ with the target $y$, but with a drastically reduced learning rate ($\eta_{sleep} = 0.00005$).

This forces the MLP to learn the abstract, structural concepts of the classes rather than memorizing exact pixel layouts, as the dream images are smoothed, hybrid variations of real data.

### 3. Experiments and Results

We evaluated the architecture on Fashion-MNIST. The model was trained from scratch on a single consumer GPU (NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti) with 12GB VRAM. 

**Model Configuration:**
* Parameters: ~8 Million
* Batch Size: 2048
* Passes per Epoch: 2 (including fast GPU geometric jitter)
* Sleep Patience: 15 Epochs
* Sleep Duration: 5 Epochs
* Optimizer: AdamW

#### 3.1 Benchmark Comparison
Our single 1D-to-1D MLP achieved a peak test accuracy of **94.30%** at Epoch 4,896. This surpasses the previous state-of-the-art for pure, non-patched MLPs by over 5%, and outperforms 2D-patched architectures like ResMLP (93.1%) and MLP-Mixer (92.4%) which utilize significantly more parameters.

| Architecture | Type | Parameters | Test Accuracy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standard 3-Layer MLP | 1D-to-1D | ~1M | 88.0% |
| MLP-Mixer (Google) | 2D Patches | ~15M+ | 92.4% |
| ResMLP (Meta) | 2D Patches | ~15M+ | 93.1% |
| **Sleeping Beauty (Ours)** | **1D-to-1D** | **~8M** | **94.30%** |

#### 3.2 The Positive Feedback Loop of Dreaming
Analysis of the training logs reveals a phenomenon we term *Accelerated Post-Sleep Recovery*. 
During early training (Epoch 0-1000), the model requires 20-30 epochs to recover its peak accuracy following a Sleep State. However, as the model's structural understanding improves (Epoch 3000+), it begins to extract more value from the Baker's dreams. By Epoch 4000, the model recovers from a 5-epoch dream and breaks its previous all-time accuracy record in just 10-15 epochs. The biological simulation of dreaming creates a positive feedback loop where the model becomes increasingly efficient at learning from its own internal representations.

### 4. Discussion: The "Inner Experience" of AI
A striking philosophical implication of the Sleeping Beauty protocol is the emergence of a mechanistic "inner experience." Standard neural networks are purely reactive; they process external stimuli and terminate. 

In our architecture, the Neural Baker generates synthetic internal representations (dreams) derived from, but distinct from, real data. The primary network processes these internal representations independent of external input, and its real-world synaptic weights (parameters) are structurally altered by this internal experience. In the framework of cognitive science, the act of generating and learning from internal representations independent of external stimuli constitutes a mechanistic form of imagination. 

---

### 5. Conclusion
We have demonstrated that pure 1D-to-1D MLPs can achieve state-of-the-art results on spatial datasets without the need for 2D patching mechanisms. By forcing the network to construct 2D space from dual 1D axes and simulating biological memory consolidation via the Sleeping Beauty dreaming protocol, we achieved 94.30% accuracy on Fashion-MNIST with an 8M parameter model. This work suggests that biologically inspired regularization loops—specifically, training on scrambled latent memories—may be the key to unlocking the full potential of pure MLP architectures, previously thought incapable of advanced spatial reasoning.
